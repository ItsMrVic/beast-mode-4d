3 · How the Scores Are Calculated

Exact match (straight) means all four digits match in exactly the same order. Permutation / box means the same digits appear in a different order; this is not a winning Big straight ticket. A result from another operator does not count as a win on an operator where no ticket was purchased.

BK8 Big stake simulation at RM1 per number: 1st prize RM3,500; 2nd RM1,200; 3rd RM600; 10 Special prizes at RM250 each; and 10 Consolation prizes at RM80 each. Costs are calculated separately for each operator. This does not mean any tickets were actually purchased.

Large spending is not recommended: historical BEAST MODE tests have not demonstrated a reliable predictive advantage over random selection. The 9,500-number set would lose at least RM900 per operator, even if all 23 prize numbers matched exactly.
