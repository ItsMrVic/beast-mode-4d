2 · Rolling prediction evaluation

Predictions recorded before the target calendar day are pre-day verifiable. Same-day entries are evaluated provisionally because the backup does not establish a draw cutoff. Permutations are observations, not confirmed ticket wins. Cross-operator results are shown separately. The random expectation assumes independent uniformly random four-digit outcomes; real draw rules may differ.
